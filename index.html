Lernova

Lernova is an Android learning app that brings AI-led lessons, practice exams, teacher exams and live rooms together in one place. Students and teachers each have their own login.

AI classes by grade

Choose a grade and open a weekly class schedule. Each day has one topic, taught every 2 hours, and the schedule moves on through the whole grade. All class times use New York time, so learners in every country see the same schedule.

A complete AI lesson

A written explanation that can be read aloud, a quiz from easy to hard, results with a worked explanation for every answer, personal feedback on your learning level, a place to ask questions, and a short summary.

Practice exams

Fixed exam questions, the same for every learner. Choose 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50 questions split evenly into easy, medium and hard. You get a score, a full explanation for every answer, and personal AI feedback on your strengths and weak points. Available now: Grade 10 Geometry, and the Sets chapter of Iran grade 9 in Persian. Persian questions are translated automatically into your language.

Exams from your teacher

A teacher uploads a PDF of questions. The app turns it into 4-option questions, the teacher checks the correct answers, then sets the date, the hour and the duration. The teacher shares the exam code. Students open "Join an exam with a code", and the exam opens at the scheduled time with a countdown timer. At the end the student sees the score and the correct answers.

Tools for teachers

Create a meeting room with a name of your choice, such as a class title, shown as a hashtag, and share its join code. Keep a day and hour schedule of your sessions on your phone. Create exams from a PDF as described above.

Live rooms

Instructors create a room and share its join code so others can join as guests. Learners can also browse public rooms. Inside a room, viewers can ask for a turn to speak; the host admits people into one of 12 speaking seats and gives them the floor.

Your language and your AI

Lernova uses your device language by default and lets you change it at any time. Gemini works inside the app. If you choose ChatGPT or Claude, the app copies your request and opens that AI app for you to continue there.
