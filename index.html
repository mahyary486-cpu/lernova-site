Lernova

Lernova is an Android learning app that brings AI-led classes, practice exams and live rooms together in one place.

AI classes by grade

Choose a grade from 10 to 12 and open a weekly class schedule. Each day has one topic, taught every 2 hours, and the schedule moves on through the whole grade. All class times use New York time, so learners in every country see the same schedule.

A complete AI lesson

A lesson video, a written explanation that can be read aloud, a quiz from easy to hard, results with a worked explanation for every answer, personal feedback on your learning level, a place to ask questions, and a short summary.

Similar exams

Fixed exam questions, the same for every learner. Choose 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50 questions split evenly into easy, medium and hard. You get a score out of 100, a full explanation for every answer, and personal AI feedback on your strengths and weak points. Grade 10 Geometry is available first.

Live rooms

Instructors create a room and share an invite link so others can join instantly as guests. Learners can also browse public rooms by category or topic.

Your language and your AI

Lernova uses your device language by default and lets you change it at any time. Gemini works inside the app. ChatGPT and Claude support is coming soon.
